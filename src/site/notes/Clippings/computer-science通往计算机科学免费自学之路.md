## Summary概括

The OSSU curriculum is a **complete education in computer science** using online materials. It's not merely for career training or professional development. It's for those who want a proper, *well-rounded* grounding in concepts fundamental to all computing disciplines, and for those who have the discipline, will, and (most importantly!) good habits to obtain this education largely on their own, but with support from a worldwide community of fellow learners.  
OSSU 的课程是一套 **完整的计算机科学教育体系，** 全部采用在线学习资源。它并非仅仅为了职业培训或专业发展，而是面向那些希望全面、 *系统地* 掌握所有计算机学科基本概念的人，以及那些拥有自律、意志和（最重要的是！）良好学习习惯的人，他们能够在来自全球学习者社群的支持下，主要依靠自身努力完成学业。

---

It is designed according to the degree requirements of undergraduate computer science majors, minus general education (non-CS) requirements, as it is assumed most of the people following this curriculum are already educated outside the field of CS.  
该课程是根据计算机科学专业本科生的学位要求设计的，减去通识教育（非计算机科学）要求，因为假设大多数学习该课程的人已经接受过计算机科学领域以外的教育。  
The courses themselves are among the very best in the world, often coming from Harvard, Princeton, MIT, etc., but specifically chosen to meet the following criteria.  
这些课程本身都是世界上最好的课程之一，通常来自哈佛大学、普林斯顿大学、麻省理工学院等，但都是经过精心挑选，以满足以下标准。

---

**Courses must**:  
**课程必须** ：

- Be open for enrollment  
	开放招生
- Run regularly (ideally in self-paced format, otherwise running multiple times per year)  
	定期跑步（理想情况下以自主配速的方式进行，否则每年跑几次）
- Be of generally high quality in teaching materials and pedagogical principles  
	教学材料和教学原则总体上应具有较高质量
- Match the curricular standards of the [CS 2013](https://github.com/ossu/computer-science/blob/master/CURRICULAR_GUIDELINES.md): Curriculum Guidelines for Undergraduate Degree Programs in Computer Science  
	符合 [CS 2013](https://github.com/ossu/computer-science/blob/master/CURRICULAR_GUIDELINES.md) ：计算机科学本科课程指南的课程标准

When no course meets the above criteria, the coursework is supplemented with a book. When there are courses or books that don't fit into the curriculum but are otherwise of high quality, they belong in [extras/courses](https://github.com/ossu/computer-science/blob/master/extras/courses.md) or [extras/readings](https://github.com/ossu/computer-science/blob/master/extras/readings.md).  
如果没有课程符合上述标准，则会用书籍作为补充教材。如果某些课程或书籍不符合课程大纲的要求，但质量很高，则会将其归入 [“附加课程”](https://github.com/ossu/computer-science/blob/master/extras/courses.md) 或 [“附加阅读”](https://github.com/ossu/computer-science/blob/master/extras/readings.md) 类别。

**Organization**. The curriculum is designed as follows:  
**组织架构** 。课程设置如下：

- *Intro CS*: for students to try out CS and see if it's right for them  
	*计算机科学入门* ：让学生体验计算机科学，看看是否适合自己。
- *Core CS*: corresponds roughly to the first three years of a computer science curriculum, taking classes that all majors would be required to take  
	*核心计算机科学课程* ：大致相当于计算机科学课程的前三年，包括所有专业学生都必须学习的课程。
- *Advanced CS*: corresponds roughly to the final year of a computer science curriculum, taking electives according to the student's interests  
	*高级计算机科学* ：大致相当于计算机科学课程的最后一年，学生可以根据自己的兴趣选修课程。
- *Final Project*: a project for students to validate, consolidate, and display their knowledge, to be evaluated by their peers worldwide  
	*最终项目* ：学生通过该项目验证、巩固和展示他们的知识，并由全球同行进行评估。

As you work through courses you can enter your actual course completion dates in the `Curriculum Data` sheet and get updated completion estimates.

## Final project最终项目

Part of learning is doing. The assignments and exams for each course are to prepare you to use your knowledge to solve real-world problems.  
学习的一部分在于实践。每门课程的作业和考试都是为了让你运用所学知识解决现实世界的问题。

After you've completed Core CS and the parts of Advanced CS relevant to you, you should identify a problem that you can solve using the knowledge you've acquired. You can create something entirely new, or you can improve some tool/program that you use and wish were better.

## Congratulations恭喜！

After completing the requirements of the curriculum above, you will have completed the equivalent of a full bachelor's degree in Computer Science. Congratulations!  
完成以上课程要求后，您将获得相当于计算机科学学士学位的学分。恭喜！

What is next for you? The possibilities are boundless and overlapping:  
接下来你的计划是什么？可能性无穷无尽，而且彼此交织：

- Look for a job as a developer!  
	去找份开发人员的工作吧！
- Check out the [readings](https://github.com/ossu/computer-science/blob/master/extras/readings.md) for classic books you can read that will sharpen your skills and expand your knowledge.  
	不妨看看 [推荐](https://github.com/ossu/computer-science/blob/master/extras/readings.md) 的经典书籍，它们能够帮助你提升技能、拓展知识。
- Join a local developer meetup (e.g. via [meetup.com](https://www.meetup.com/)).  
	加入本地开发者聚会（例如通过 [meetup.com](https://www.meetup.com/) ）。
- Pay attention to emerging technologies in the world of software development:  
	关注软件开发领域的新兴技术：
	- Explore the **actor model** through [Elixir](https://elixir-lang.org/), a new functional programming language for the web based on the battle-tested Erlang Virtual Machine!  
		通过 [Elixir](https://elixir-lang.org/) 探索 **Actor 模型** ，Elixir 是一种基于久经考验的 Erlang 虚拟机的全新 Web 函数式编程语言！
		- Explore **borrowing and lifetimes** through [Rust](https://www.rust-lang.org/), a systems language which achieves memory- and thread-safety without a garbage collector!  
		通过 [Rust](https://www.rust-lang.org/) 探索 **借用和生命周期** ，Rust 是一种无需垃圾回收器即可实现内存安全和线程安全的系统语言！
		- Explore **dependent type systems** through [Idris](https://www.idris-lang.org/), a new Haskell-inspired language with unprecedented support for type-driven development.  
		通过 [Idris](https://www.idris-lang.org/) 探索 **依赖类型系统** ，Idris 是一种受 Haskell 启发的新语言，对类型驱动开发提供了前所未有的支持。

## How to show your progress如何展示你的进度

[Fork](https://www.freecodecamp.org/news/how-to-fork-a-github-repository/) the [GitHub repo](https://github.com/ossu/computer-science) into your own GitHub account and put ✅ next to the stuff you've completed as you complete it. This can serve as your [kanban board](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kanban_board) and will be faster to implement than any other solution (giving you time to spend on the courses).  
将 [GitHub 代码库](https://github.com/ossu/computer-science) [fork](https://www.freecodecamp.org/news/how-to-fork-a-github-repository/) 到你自己的 GitHub 账户，并在完成一项任务后，在任务旁边标记 ✅。这可以作为你的 [看板](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kanban_board) ，并且比任何其他解决方案都更容易实现（让你有更多时间学习课程）。
