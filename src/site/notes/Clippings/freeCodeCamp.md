## freeCodeCamp.org's open-source codebase and curriculumfreeCodeCamp.org 的开源代码库和课程

[freeCodeCamp.org](https://www.freecodecamp.org/) is a friendly community where you can learn to code for free. It is run by a [donor-supported 501(c)(3) charity](https://www.freecodecamp.org/donate) to help millions of busy adults transition into tech. Our community has already helped more than 100,000 people get their first developer job.  
[freeCodeCamp.org](https://www.freecodecamp.org/) 是一个友好的社区，您可以在这里免费学习编程。它由一家 [依靠捐赠者支持的 501(c)(3) 非营利组织](https://www.freecodecamp.org/donate) 运营，旨在帮助数百万忙碌的成年人转型进入科技行业。我们的社区已经帮助超过 10 万人找到了他们的第一份开发人员工作。

Our full-stack web development and machine learning curriculum is completely free and self-paced. We have thousands of interactive coding challenges to help you expand your skills.  
我们的全栈 Web 开发和机器学习课程完全免费，而且学习进度可自主掌控。我们提供数千个交互式编程挑战，帮助您提升技能。

### Certifications认证

freeCodeCamp.org offers several free developer certifications that make up the [Full-Stack Developer Curriculum](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/full-stack-developer-v9/):  
freeCodeCamp.org 提供多个免费的开发者认证，这些认证构成了 [全栈开发者课程](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/full-stack-developer-v9/) ：

- [Responsive Web Design  
	响应式网页设计](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/responsive-web-design-v9/)
- [JavaScript](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/javascript-v9/)
- [Front-End Development Libraries  
	前端开发库](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/front-end-development-libraries-v9/)
- [Python](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/python-v9/)
- [Relational Databases  
	关系型数据库](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/relational-databases-v9/)
- [Back-End Development and APIs  
	后端开发和 API](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/back-end-development-and-apis-v9/)

Each of these certifications involves completing interactive lessons, workshops, labs, reviews, and quizzes. Throughout the certification, you'll need to complete 5 required projects to qualify for the exam. Once you pass the exam, then you can claim the certification.  
每项认证都包含完成互动课程、研讨会、实验、复习和测验。在整个认证过程中，您需要完成 5 个必修项目才能获得考试资格。考试通过后，您即可获得认证。

freeCodeCamp.org also offers free language certifications designed around internationally recognized proficiency levels:  
freeCodeCamp.org 还提供根据国际认可的熟练程度设计的免费语言认证：

- [A2 English for Developers (Beta)  
	面向开发者的 A2 英语（测试版）](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/a2-english-for-developers/)
- [B1 English for Developers (Beta)  
	B1 英语（开发者版）（测试版）](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/b1-english-for-developers/)
- [A1 Professional Spanish (Beta)  
	A1 专业西班牙语（测试版）](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/a1-professional-spanish/)
- [A1 Professional Chinese (Beta)  
	A1 专业中文（测试版）](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/a1-professional-chinese/)

---

Each of these certifications is organized into modules, with sections for warm-ups, lessons, practice exercises, review pages, and quizzes to ensure you fully grasp the material before progressing to the next module.  
每个认证课程都分为多个模块，每个模块都包含热身、课程、练习、复习页面和测验等部分，以确保你在进入下一个模块之前完全掌握相关内容。  
You'll need to complete all of the quizzes in order to qualify for the exam at the end of the certification.  
你需要完成所有测验才能获得认证结束时的考试资格。

---

Once you've earned a certification, you will always have it. You will always be able to link to it from your LinkedIn or resume. And when your prospective employers or freelance clients click that link, they'll see a verified certification specific to you.  
获得认证后，您将永久拥有它。您可以随时在 LinkedIn 或简历中添加链接。当您的潜在雇主或自由职业客户点击该链接时，他们就能看到专属于您的认证信息。

The one exception to this is if we discover violations of our [Academic Honesty Policy](https://www.freecodecamp.org/news/academic-honesty-policy/). When we catch people unambiguously plagiarizing (submitting other people's code or projects as their own without citation), we do what all rigorous institutions of learning should do - we revoke their certifications and ban those people.  
唯一的例外情况是，如果我们发现有人违反了我们的 [学术诚信政策](https://www.freecodecamp.org/news/academic-honesty-policy/) 。当我们发现有人明确抄袭（提交他人的代码或项目，且未注明出处）时，我们会采取所有严谨的学术机构都应该采取的措施——吊销其证书并禁止其入学。

In addition, to help prepare for job interviews, freeCodeCamp.org includes The Odin Project (freeCodeCamp Remix), Coding Interview Prep, Project Euler, and Rosetta Code.  
此外，为了帮助求职者准备面试，freeCodeCamp.org 还包括 Odin 项目（freeCodeCamp Remix）、编程面试准备、Project Euler 和 Rosetta Code。

A free, professional Foundational C# with Microsoft Certification is also available.  
我们还提供免费的、专业的、带有微软认证的 C# 基础课程。

### Reporting Bugs and Issues报告错误和问题

If you think you've found a bug, first read the [how to report a bug](https://forum.freecodecamp.org/t/how-to-report-a-bug/19543) article and follow its instructions.  
如果您认为自己发现了一个漏洞，请先阅读 [如何报告漏洞的](https://forum.freecodecamp.org/t/how-to-report-a-bug/19543) 文章并按照其中的说明进行操作。

If you're confident it's a new bug and have confirmed that someone else is facing the same issue, go ahead and create a new GitHub issue. Be sure to include as much information as possible so we can reproduce the bug.  
如果您确信这是一个新 bug，并且已经确认其他人也遇到了同样的问题，请创建一个新的 GitHub issue。请务必提供尽可能多的信息，以便我们能够重现该 bug。

We appreciate responsible disclosure of vulnerabilities that might impact the integrity of our platforms and users.  
我们赞赏对可能影响我们平台和用户完整性的漏洞进行负责任的披露。

> #### Read our security policy and follow these steps to report a vulnerability.请阅读我们的安全策略，并按照以下步骤报告漏洞 。
