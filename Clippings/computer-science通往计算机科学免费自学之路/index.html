Open Source Society University开源社会大学

Path to a free self-taught education in Computer Science!
通往计算机科学免费自学之路！

Contents内容

Summary概括

The OSSU curriculum is a complete education in computer science using online materials. It's not merely for career training or professional development. It's for those who want a proper, well-rounded grounding in concepts fundamental to all computing disciplines, and for those who have the discipline, will, and (most importantly!) good habits to obtain this education largely on their own, but with support from a worldwide community of fellow learners.
OSSU 的课程是一套 完整的计算机科学教育体系， 全部采用在线学习资源。它并非仅仅为了职业培训或专业发展，而是面向那些希望全面、 系统地 掌握所有计算机学科基本概念的人，以及那些拥有自律、意志和（最重要的是！）良好学习习惯的人，他们能够在来自全球学习者社群的支持下，主要依靠自身努力完成学业。

It is designed according to the degree requirements of undergraduate computer science majors, minus general education (non-CS) requirements, as it is assumed most of the people following this curriculum are already educated outside the field of CS.
该课程是根据计算机科学专业本科生的学位要求设计的，减去通识教育（非计算机科学）要求，因为假设大多数学习该课程的人已经接受过计算机科学领域以外的教育。
The courses themselves are among the very best in the world, often coming from Harvard, Princeton, MIT, etc., but specifically chosen to meet the following criteria.
这些课程本身都是世界上最好的课程之一，通常来自哈佛大学、普林斯顿大学、麻省理工学院等，但都是经过精心挑选，以满足以下标准。

Courses must:
课程必须 ：

  • Be open for enrollment
    开放招生
  • Run regularly (ideally in self-paced format, otherwise running multiple times per year)
    定期跑步（理想情况下以自主配速的方式进行，否则每年跑几次）
  • Be of generally high quality in teaching materials and pedagogical principles
    教学材料和教学原则总体上应具有较高质量
  • Match the curricular standards of the CS 2013: Curriculum Guidelines for Undergraduate Degree Programs in Computer Science
    符合 CS 2013 ：计算机科学本科课程指南的课程标准

When no course meets the above criteria, the coursework is supplemented with a book. When there are courses or books that don't fit into the curriculum but are otherwise of high quality, they belong in extras/courses or extras/readings.
如果没有课程符合上述标准，则会用书籍作为补充教材。如果某些课程或书籍不符合课程大纲的要求，但质量很高，则会将其归入 “附加课程” 或 “附加阅读” 类别。

Organization. The curriculum is designed as follows:
组织架构 。课程设置如下：

  • Intro CS: for students to try out CS and see if it's right for them
    计算机科学入门 ：让学生体验计算机科学，看看是否适合自己。
  • Core CS: corresponds roughly to the first three years of a computer science curriculum, taking classes that all majors would be required to take
    核心计算机科学课程 ：大致相当于计算机科学课程的前三年，包括所有专业学生都必须学习的课程。
  • Advanced CS: corresponds roughly to the final year of a computer science curriculum, taking electives according to the student's interests
    高级计算机科学 ：大致相当于计算机科学课程的最后一年，学生可以根据自己的兴趣选修课程。
  • Final Project: a project for students to validate, consolidate, and display their knowledge, to be evaluated by their peers worldwide
    最终项目 ：学生通过该项目验证、巩固和展示他们的知识，并由全球同行进行评估。

Duration. It is possible to finish within about 2 years if you plan carefully and devote roughly 20 hours/week to your studies. Learners can use this spreadsheet to estimate their end date. Make a copy and input your start date and expected hours per week in the Timeline sheet. As you work through courses you can enter your actual course completion dates in the Curriculum Data sheet and get updated completion estimates.
时长 ：如果您精心规划并每周投入大约 20 小时学习，大约可以在两年内完成。学习者可以使用 此电子表格 估算完成日期。复制一份，并在 Timeline 表中输入您的开始日期和预计每周学习时长。随着课程的进行，您可以将实际的课程完成日期输入到 Curriculum Data 表中，并获取更新后的完成时间估算。

Warning: While the spreadsheet is a useful tool to estimate the time you need to complete this curriculum, it may not always be up-to-date with the curriculum. Use the OSSU CS website or the repo to see what courses to do.
警告： 虽然此电子表格是估算完成课程所需时间的有用工具，但它可能并非始终与课程内容保持同步更新。请使用 OSSU 计算机科学系网站 或 代码库 查看具体课程信息。

Cost. All or nearly all course material is available for free. However, some courses may charge money for assignments/tests/projects to be graded. Note that both Coursera and edX offer financial aid.
费用 。几乎所有课程资料均可免费获取。但是，部分课程可能会对作业/考试/项目收取费用。请注意， Coursera 和 edX 都提供经济援助。

Decide how much or how little to spend based on your own time and budget; just remember that you can't purchase success!
根据你自己的时间和预算来决定投入多少；但请记住，成功是买不到的！

Process. Students can work through the curriculum alone or in groups, in order or out of order.
过程 。学生可以单独学习课程，也可以分组学习，可以按顺序学习，也可以不按顺序学习。

  • We recommend doing all courses in Core CS, only skipping a course when you are certain that you've already learned the material previously.
    我们建议完成所有核心计算机科学课程，只有当你确定自己之前已经学过相关内容时，才可以跳过某门课程。
  • For simplicity, we recommend working through courses (especially Core CS) in order from top to bottom. Some students choose to study multiple courses at a time in order to vary the material they are working on in a day/week.
    为了方便起见，我们建议按顺序学习课程（尤其是核心计算机科学课程）。有些学生选择同时学习多门课程，以便每天/每周学习的内容更加多样化。
    A popular option is to take the math courses in parallel with the introductory courses. Course prerequisites are listed to help you determine if you are prepared for a given course.
    一种常见的做法是同时修读数学课程和入门课程。课程先修要求已列出，以帮助您确定自己是否已做好修读特定课程的准备。
  • Courses in Advanced CS are electives. Choose one subject (e.g. Advanced programming) you want to become an expert in and take all the courses under that heading. You can also create your own custom subject; the Discord community may provide feedback on your planned subject.
    高级计算机科学课程均为选修课。选择一个你想精通的主题（例如高级编程），并修读该主题下的所有课程。你也可以创建自己的自定义主题；Discord 社区可能会就你计划的主题提供反馈。

Content policy. If you plan on showing off some of your coursework publicly, you must share only files that you are allowed to. Respect the code of conduct that you signed in the beginning of each course!
内容政策 。如果您计划公开展示部分课程作业，则必须仅分享您有权分享的文件。 请遵守你在每门课程开始时签署的行为准则 ！

Final project最终项目

Part of learning is doing. The assignments and exams for each course are to prepare you to use your knowledge to solve real-world problems.
学习的一部分在于实践。每门课程的作业和考试都是为了让你运用所学知识解决现实世界的问题。

After you've completed Core CS and the parts of Advanced CS relevant to you, you should identify a problem that you can solve using the knowledge you've acquired. You can create something entirely new, or you can improve some tool/program that you use and wish were better.
完成核心计算机科学课程和与你相关的进阶计算机科学课程后，你应该找到一个可以用所学知识解决的问题。你可以创造全新的事物，也可以改进你正在使用但希望做得更好的工具/程序。

What is next for you? The possibilities are boundless and overlapping:
接下来你的计划是什么？可能性无穷无尽，而且彼此交织：

  • Look for a job as a developer!
    去找份开发人员的工作吧！
  • Check out the readings for classic books you can read that will sharpen your skills and expand your knowledge.
    不妨看看 推荐 的经典书籍，它们能够帮助你提升技能、拓展知识。

    加入本地开发者聚会（例如通过 meetup.com ）。
  • Pay attention to emerging technologies in the world of software development:
    关注软件开发领域的新兴技术：
    • Explore the actor model through Elixir, a new functional programming language for the web based on the battle-tested Erlang Virtual Machine!
      通过 Elixir 探索 Actor 模型 ，Elixir 是一种基于久经考验的 Erlang 虚拟机的全新 Web 函数式编程语言！

        通过 Rust 探索 借用和生命周期 ，Rust 是一种无需垃圾回收器即可实现内存安全和线程安全的系统语言！

        通过 Idris 探索 依赖类型系统 ，Idris 是一种受 Haskell 启发的新语言，对类型驱动开发提供了前所未有的支持。

How to show your progress如何展示你的进度

Fork the GitHub repo into your own GitHub account and put ✅ next to the stuff you've completed as you complete it. This can serve as your kanban board and will be faster to implement than any other solution (giving you time to spend on the courses).
将 GitHub 代码库 fork 到你自己的 GitHub 账户，并在完成一项任务后，在任务旁边标记 ✅。这可以作为你的 看板 ，并且比任何其他解决方案都更容易实现（让你有更多时间学习课程）。
